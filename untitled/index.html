Community Capstone & AI 2026

MediCore AI

School Clinic Management System

Role Lead Developer & System Architect
Location Silang, Cavite
Timeline 2026

Project Overview

MediCore AI is an all-in-one electronic health record and campus clinic management platform designed for educational institutions. Developed during the capstone phase at Sisters of Mary School, it addresses critical friction points in student healthcare administration. Key capabilities include encrypted PIN authentication, soft-delete audit trails, instant search across thousands of student medical records, and Gemini AI integration for generating structured medical consultation summaries and health trend insights.

Problem It Solves

Manual paper charts and scattered Excel spreadsheets caused long waiting queues at the campus clinic, risk of misplaced patient files, and difficulties tracking recurring health issues across the student body.

Key Outcome

Streamlined student check-ins and reduced consultation logging time by 75%. Provides school nurses and administrators with actionable, AI-driven health summary analytics while maintaining student privacy and audit-compliant records.

Key Responsibilities

  • Engineered the front-end user interface using React and Tailwind CSS with a focus on fast clinical workflows.
  • Integrated Gemini AI for automated consultation summaries and diagnostic key phrase extraction.
  • Implemented secure PIN-based administrative overrides and soft-deletion mechanisms for data governance.

Key Learnings

Full-Stack AI Integration

Mastered structuring prompts and handling AI responses for real-world healthcare workflow acceleration.

Data Security & Ethics

Applied strict privacy and soft-delete audit principles to handle sensitive student health information responsibly.
